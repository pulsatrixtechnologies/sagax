## Put a bot on the desktop

Right-click a bot in the sidebar and choose **Put on the desktop**. The bot leaves the window and stands on your desktop as its mascot, above your other apps. In a browser or on a phone it floats over the app instead. Choose **Put back in the window** from its right-click menu to bring it home.

## Choose its character

Each bot picks its character in **Edit persona > Appearance** (click the big avatar in the bot's panel to open it there). Under **Mascot**, a gallery lists the characters with a search and filters (All, Owned, To unlock, and one per family: Classics, Shapes, Animals); the column beside it previews the one you pick with its colors, skins and moves. **Generate** and **Upload** set a picture instead, and **Reset** brings the owl back. By default the bot can wear the **Owl**, with its colors, skins and wing moves. **Shapes** (forty of our own shapes, such as the circle, the pebble, the pumpkin, the cup or the ship, then the open-source avatar gallery's: a brain, a bull, a bunny, a camera, a magnifier, a paintbrush, a palette, a witch hat, a barbell and a watering can; each a body in the bot's color with volume and two eyes, drawn by the open-bot-avatar engine; the big mascot on the bot page and in Appearance breathes, blinks and follows your pointer, thinks while the bot works and smiles when it is done; the small ones in lists stay still; a skin and fourteen moves such as Thinking, Wink, Orbit or Comet) appear after you link a Grok account. A bot that wore one of the earlier shapes now shows the nearest one. **Trombi** and **Bunbu** (our own little collectible monster with long ears and a toothy grin, in the bot's color and a Bunbu skin; its signature move is the ear flop) appear when you unlock them. **Shiba** (a dog drawn in flat tones with one outline, in the bot's color or a coat of its own, with sixteen faces) is a Mastery character: the achievement Hands Off unlocks it, and its coats and premium skins come from the Mastery achievements (see Achievements). **Frog** (a smug, heavy-lidded frog with wide flat lips, its green taking a tint of the bot's color, with sixteen faces) is one too: Polyglot unlocks it. A locked character shows on the **To unlock** shelf with the achievement that unlocks it and how far you are: you can preview it, not wear it yet (Trombi stays a secret until you find it). A locked skin stays out of the list until then. A bot that already wears one keeps it. The preview plays the moves you pick and the equip animation of a skin you choose. The choice is stored with the bot, so it shows everywhere the bot appears (the sidebar, the chat, the panels, the desktop mascot) and on your other devices.

Skins come in four rarities, one tab each in the popover (with how many skins it holds), and the popover opens on the tab of the current skin: Common (Owl: classic, snowy, barn, carbon; Shapes: clay, pastel, glossy, night; Trombi: classic, retro 98; Bunbu: plain, pastel, night, plush), Rare (Owl: gold, ice; Shapes: outline, gold; Trombi: gold; Bunbu: velvet, gold), Epic (Owl: neon, lightning, chrome; Shapes: neon, chrome, crystal, circuit; Trombi: neon, chrome, glitch; Bunbu: neon, chrome, crystal) and Legendary (Owl: inferno, holographic, galaxy, spirit; Shapes: holographic, molten, galaxy; Trombi: holographic, molten; Bunbu: holographic, galaxy, molten). Shiba wears the bot's color (Plain) for free; its coats (cream, black and tan, red, sesame, white) and Retro 98 and Gold are Rare, Neon, Chrome and Glitch Epic, Holographic and Molten Legendary. Frog wears the bot's tint (Plain) for free; its real frogs (leaf, tree frog with red eyes and orange toes, poison dart, bullfrog, ghost) and Retro 98 and Gold are Rare, Neon, Chrome and Glitch Epic, Holographic and Molten Legendary. The phone draws Shiba and Frog from the same art, with the base look of a premium skin. The iPhone app shows a Bunbu bot as the owl for now. A premium skin has an idle effect, an equip animation and its own effects on every move (trails, particles, shockwaves). Small avatars such as the sidebar show its still look; with Reduce motion on, every skin keeps its look and nothing moves or flashes. Each character keeps its own skin when you switch and come back.

Colors come in five palettes, one tab each: Vivid (the original bright colors), Pastel, Deep, Neon and Neutral (white, greys, black, sand, browns and metals). Shapes adds a sixth, Clay (twelve colors made for the clay finish). The popover opens on the palette of the bot's color, and a bot keeps its color from any earlier version. Every color keeps the eyes readable (a shape's eyes are holes, so the page behind shows through). The Shape grid previews each shape in the current color and skin.

## Talk to it

- **Click** the mascot, or press **Enter** while it has focus, to open its balloon, ready to type. The reply streams into the balloon; **Open in Sagax** jumps to the full thread.
- **Double-click** it to open Sagax on the bot's thread.
- The balloon grows with the answer (up to about 60 % of its screen, then it scrolls, earlier exchanges above) and never runs off the screen: it opens on the side of the mascot that has room, below it near the top of a screen and to its right near the left edge, and stays on the mascot's screen. It shows lists, bold, code, tables and links like the chat, your messages in the same bubbles. Its composer is the app's: the paperclip and the model chip open the app on the bot's thread (its file picker, its model picker), the voice button starts a call. Drag its corner grip to resize it, drag its header to move it anywhere, right up against the mascot (it never covers it), and use the pin button to put it back; its size and place are remembered per bot. **Enter** sends, **Shift+Enter** starts a new line, **Esc** closes it. With Trombi it wears the Windows 98 look.
- **Call** it: where voice mode is on for the bot, the voice button in the balloon's composer (or **Start a voice call** in the right-click menu) starts a voice call through the bot's thread, the same call as in the app. A small call pill stands under the mascot: the live waveform, Settings, Transcript, Mic and the red button to hang up. The mascot bounces while its bot talks and leans in while you speak (not with Reduce motion on); click it to cut the bot short. Only one call runs at a time: starting one in the app or on another mascot ends the other.
- **Click** it to chat: a plain click on the mascot opens its chat balloon and never starts a call (use the call button that shows when you hover it, or the hotkey). A drag still moves it and a double click still opens the app. During the call a click only cuts the bot short while it talks; it never hangs up (the pill's red button does). The call is the same as the app's call bar: both show it.
- **Hover** it: after a moment three round buttons appear beside it (never over it): quick chat (the balloon), voice (start or end the call) and activity, a small tray of what its bot is running now with **Allow** and **Stop** for anything waiting on you; click a row to open it in the app. They fade away a moment after the pointer leaves (without a fade with Reduce motion on).
- **Captions**: during a call the mascot shows the call's status (listening, thinking, speaking) and the live words beside it, yours as you speak and the bot's word by word as it says them, three lines at most. Turn them off in **Settings > Appearance > Call captions beside the mascot**.
- **Hotkey**: press **Control+Option+Space** (Ctrl+Alt+Space on Windows and Linux) anywhere to call the mascot's bot, press again to mute or unmute, and, when the call is set to push to talk, hold it to talk (macOS). Change the keys or turn it off in **Settings > Appearance > Call hotkey**; it is off while no mascot is on the desktop.
- **Right-click** it (or press the menu key or **Shift+F10** while it has focus) for its menu: **Talk**, **Start a voice call**, **Open in Sagax**, **Switch bot** (the same mascot, in the same spot, for another of your bots), **Moves** (its character's moves), **Hide for 1 hour** (it comes back by itself), **Hide** (back in the window), **On the desktop** (always on top, fly away during tasks, activity level) and **Settings…**.
- **Drag** it anywhere, on any screen, right into a corner or against an edge: it flaps and kicks while you carry it and lands with a wobble. It stays fully visible and keeps its spot per screen setup; it only goes back to its first spot when that screen is gone. Its reactions, signs and hearts appear beside it, never over it.

## Play with it

The mascot has a little life of its own: it breathes and blinks, follows your pointer with its eyes, looks around, preens, scratches, pecks, stretches a wing, hoots, walks a few steps along the desk (turning to face the way it goes) and now and then hops or flies a short way. After a quiet while it yawns and naps until you come back.

- **Stroke** it by moving the pointer back and forth over it: it hugs itself and leans into your hand; keep going and it shows hearts.
- Rush the pointer past it and it jumps; rest the pointer near it and it waves.

### Shiba on the desktop

Shiba lives like a dog. It sits, breathes, blinks, wags its tail and twitches an ear, and now and then it sniffs, lies down for a nap, turns in circles or barks. Every few minutes it wanders: it walks off to a spot (always within its room on the screen, never while its chat is open), sniffs it, turns around and walks back. It walks on four legs while the mascot moves along the desk and runs while it flies off for a task.

- It **barks** when someone nudges you, **turns in circles** when you unlock an achievement and gets **excited** when a reply lands.
- It **sits up**, head tilted, while its bot waits for your approval, and droops its ears after an error.
- **Hide for 1 hour** makes it lie down and fall asleep before it goes.
- Drop it somewhere new and it goes to sniff the spot, then comes back to it.
- Its **Moves** menu (and the Appearance gallery) offers Bark, Turn in circles, Wag, Sniff, Head tilt, Stretch, Lie down, Shake off, Wave and Excited.
- Its bark is silent unless you turn on **Settings > Appearance > Shiba barks out loud**.

With Reduce motion on it stays still and a move only changes its face.

### Frog on the desktop

Frog sits with its heavy lids half closed, breathes with its throat and blinks one eye, then the other. Now and then it catches a fly, nods smugly or croaks. Every few minutes it hops about, and more rarely it takes a long jump to another spot on the screen (never while its chat is open). It hops along while the mascot moves along the desk and long-jumps while it flies off for a task.

- It **puffs its throat** while its bot works.
- It gives a slow **side-eye**, then **sinks into its pond** up to the eyes while its bot waits for your approval, and **shivers** after a refusal or an error.
- It **nods smugly** when a task goes well.
- It **croaks** when someone nudges you, **celebrates** an achievement and **catches a fly** with its tongue when a reply lands.
- It **naps on a lily pad** and **stretches its legs** when it wakes up.
- Its **Moves** menu (and the Appearance gallery) offers Hop, Long jump, Catch a fly, Croak, Smug nod, Lazy blink, Stretch, Side-eye, Shiver and Wave.

With Reduce motion on it stays still and a move only changes its face (the pond and the lily pad still show).

## Fly away during tasks

When the bot starts working, the mascot crouches, spreads its wings and flies to the nearest edge of its screen, flapping all the way, and waits there as a small badge with a turning ring. Click the badge to open the thread. When the work is done it flies back to the spot it left, lands and celebrates; if a message could not be sent it comes back looking sad, and if the bot needs your approval it comes straight back.

Turn this off in **App settings → Appearance → Fly away during tasks**, or from the mascot's right-click menu. With it off, the mascot stays put and thinks while the bot works.

## Activity level

**App settings → Appearance → Activity level** (also in the right-click menu) sets how often the mascot moves on its own: **Calm**, **Normal** or **Lively**.

## Motion and battery

- With **Reduce motion** on in your system settings, the mascot stays calm: no walks, flights or big moves, only breathing, blinking and gentle looks.
- It draws only as fast as it needs, and not at all while hidden or covered.
- Clicks pass through the empty space around the mascot to whatever is underneath.
